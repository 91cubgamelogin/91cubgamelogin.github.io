Privacy policy

Your visit, explained plainly

This policy describes what this informational website handles, what its hosting providers may process and the choices available to visitors.

Last updated: 2 September 2026
01

Information you provide

This static website does not require visitors to register, create a profile, make a payment or provide gaming-account credentials. Never send passwords, OTPs, card PINs or full banking information when reporting a correction or asking a general question.

02

Technical information

The services that host and deliver this website may process basic technical data such as an IP address, browser type, device type, requested page, date and time. This information may be used for security, reliability, abuse prevention and aggregate service measurement under the provider's own terms.

03

Cookies and local storage

The current website does not intentionally set advertising or personalised-marketing cookies. Its infrastructure may use essential technologies needed to deliver pages securely. If new analytics or interactive features are introduced, this policy will be updated before those tools are used.

04

External links

A link to another website takes you outside 91 Guidebook. That destination controls its own content, security and privacy practices. Review the destination address and policy before entering personal or financial information. A link is not a guarantee or endorsement.

05

Children's privacy

This website is intended only for adults aged 18 and older. It is not designed to attract or knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent information should use the contact page to request review.

06

Retention, security and your choices

We do not maintain a visitor account database through this static site. No internet service can promise absolute security, so share only the minimum information needed. You may choose not to follow external links and can use browser controls to manage site data.

07

Policy changes and questions

This page may change when the website's features or legal obligations change. The updated date at the top will show the latest revision. Questions or correction requests can be sent through the method listed on the Contact page.
