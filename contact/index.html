Contact us

Send useful feedback, safely

Contact this independent publication about corrections, accessibility issues or unclear information. For account or payment problems, use the verified support channel of the relevant service.

Public feedback

Use the website repository

The public repository's Issues area is the official route for page corrections and accessibility reports. Please describe the page and the exact change you recommend.

Open GitHub Repository link being connected

Safe to include

  • The page title or section name
  • A short description of the error
  • A reliable public source for a correction
  • An accessibility problem and your device type

Never include

  • Password, PIN or one-time code
  • Full phone, bank or card details
  • Identity documents or payment screenshots
  • Money sent as a “support fee”

This is not customer support

91 Guidebook cannot access a gaming account, reverse a transaction, recover a password or approve a withdrawal. Do not post private account information in a public issue. If you believe you are facing fraud or immediate financial harm, contact your payment provider and an appropriate local authority.
